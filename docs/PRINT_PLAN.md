# C.A.R.L. print plan (Bambu Lab A1, PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle)

Total printed material: about 550 cm3 solid, roughly 350-420 g of PETG. One 1 kg spool is enough.
Settings: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 25-30 % infill (gyroid), glue stick on a smooth PEI plate. Bed 256 x 256 mm.
Wait for the shaft fit-test result and the wheel bore measurement before printing the two hub adapters (08) and the front bushes (10).

| Order | Plate | Parts (file numbers in cad/stl) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lower deck | 01 lower deck main (160 x 215) alone | Biggest part, everything bolts to it. Check bed adhesion first. |
| 2 | Front end | 02 nose, 03 splice plate, 11 tie rod (156 long), 14 bumper | Steering and the ToF mount |
| 3 | Drivetrain | 06 motor saddles x2, 07 straps x4, 09 knuckles x2, 10 wheel bushes x2 (after wheel check) | Gets you a rolling chassis |
| 4 | Hubs and skirts | 08 hub adapters x2 (after fit test), 12 side skirts x2, 05 outriggers x2, 13 steering-servo pillars x2 | Finishing the chassis |
| 5 | Upper deck | 04 upper deck | UNO Q posts and the pan-servo slot |
| 6 | Gimbal | 15 pan plate, 17 tilt cradle, 16 tilt uprights x2, 18 pivot bush x2 | Print last, needs the servos in hand |

After plate 3 you can run the car on the floor without the gimbal. Plates 1 to 4 print in roughly 12 to 16 hours in total; the A1 slicer will give the exact figure.
Print the knuckles and the uprights so the load runs along the layers, not across them: they are exported already lying in that orientation.
